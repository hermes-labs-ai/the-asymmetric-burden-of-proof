## Source recovery state

An exact manuscript or LaTeX source that is proven to have generated the
published PDF was not located during this pass. Two source families were found:

- earlier website Markdown with an older model label, incomplete-condition
  treatment, and a narrower reported gap range; and
- working Word drafts associated with the evaluation-data handoff.

Neither is included here because neither is byte- or text-equivalent to the
published paper. The absence of an included source means “not proven recovered
in this pass,” not “the source never existed.”

The published PDF remains authoritative. Any later source recovery must be
added as a separately named artifact with its own checksum and a documented
comparison; it must not replace the PDF.
